# The name and the icons

The code is GPL-3.0 ([LICENSE](LICENSE)). The license covers the code only. The names "Lumbina"
and "Lumbina TV Provider", the icons and the banner are not licensed and stay with the author.

In practice:

- Unchanged builds. You can share the released APKs and your own builds of unmodified source
  under the name Lumbina.
- Changed versions. A fork or a modified build needs another name, another icon and another
  application id (`dev.lumbina.tv` and `dev.lumbina.tv.tvprovider` belong to the project). It
  must not present itself as Lumbina. "Based on Lumbina" is fine.
- Stores. Please don't publish an app named Lumbina or with its icon in an app store. The project
  does that itself.

Lumbina is not affiliated with Hisense, Vidda, Xiaomi, Google or any other maker of the devices
and apps it works with. Their names are used only to say what Lumbina runs on.
